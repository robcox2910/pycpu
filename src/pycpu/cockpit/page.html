The Cockpit

A whole computer made of NAND gates, with the lid off. Run a Pebble program, pause it, step it a gate, an instruction or a line at a time, and click any value to zoom down through the circuit to the single gates that hold it. Everything shown is read from the real machine; a wire carrying a 1 is highlighted.
